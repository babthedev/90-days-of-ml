# 90-Day Spanish Roadmap: A1 to B2 Track 🇪🇸

A structured, 90-day intensive program (Oct 1 – Dec 29, 2026) compressing 6 months of curriculum into 90 paired daily blocks (120 min/day).

---

## 🎯 Realistic Expectations & CEFR Math
- **Total Hours:** 90 days × 2 hours/day = **180 hours**.
- **Realistic Outcome:** A rock-solid, functional **A2+ / pushing into B1** with active speaking fluency and complete grammatical coverage of the B2 syllabus.
- **Phase 6 Purpose:** The DELE B2 mock exams serve as diagnostic tools to highlight your exact growth areas and prepare you for subsequent B1/B2 certifications.
