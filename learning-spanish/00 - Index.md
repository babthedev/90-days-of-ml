## ⏱️ Daily 120-Minute Block Structure
| Block | Time | Focus | Non-Negotiable Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grammar** | 35 min | Topic explanation & drill | 20+ sentences written |
| **Vocabulary** | 25 min | Anki spaced repetition | Reviews first + 30 new words |
| **Speaking** | 20 min | Free spoken output (recorded) | 15+ min speaking, no scripts |
| **Listening** | 25 min | Graded input (CBS, Dreaming Spanish, Hoy Hablamos) | Catch verbs & time markers |
| **Writing** | 15 min | Diary / Essay / Email | Corrected & logged in [[ERROR-LOG]] |

---

## 🎙️ Live Conversations (From Day 8)
- 15 minutes, 3–4 times per week via **italki** (tutors) or **Tandem** (exchange partners).
- Focus on unscripted communication, listening to native speed, and taking real-time corrections.
