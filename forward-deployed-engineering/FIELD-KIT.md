## 1. 20-Question Discovery Bank (Day 3)
### Core Pain & Process
1. Walk me through the last time you had to do [process]?
2. How many hours a week does your team spend on this?
3. What makes this task frustrating or slow?
4. What happens when an error occurs in this process?
5. How do you currently track or verify this work?

### Stakeholders & Decision Making
6. Who initiates this task, who does the work, and who reviews the final output?
7. Who would need to approve a change to this workflow?
8. If this works perfectly, what does success look like for you personally?
9. Is there anyone on the team who might be hesitant about automating this?
10. What other tools or integrations does this touch?

### Technical & System Constraints
11. Where does the source data live (CRM, Google Sheets, email, custom DB)?
12. How are permissions currently handled?
13. What security or compliance standards must we comply with?
14. What level of API access or service accounts can we obtain?
15. What are the rate limits or uptime requirements?

### Budget & Urgency
16. Have you tried solving this before? What happened?
17. If we don't solve this this quarter, what is the consequence?
18. What would a 50% reduction in turnaround time mean for your team?
19. What is your timeline for having a live solution in place?
20. What is the process for signing off on a completed milestone?

## 4. Difficult Moments Scripts (Day 78)
- **Scope Creep Response:** "That's a fantastic idea. To ensure we hit our scheduled go-live date for the core deliverables, let's log this as Phase 2 item and scope it right after our milestone acceptance."
- **Missed Deadline Handling:** "Here is exactly what occurred, here is the mitigation in place, and here is the adjusted target delivery time with zero compromise on quality."
- **Executive Pushback:** "I understand your concern about [issue]. Here is the data on our test runs and the exact fallback mechanism we have implemented."
