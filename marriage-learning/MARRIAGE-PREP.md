## 4. Weekly Marriage Meeting Template (Days 58, 85)
```markdown
**Date:** [Date] | **Duration:** 30–45 mins

1. **Appreciation (5 mins):** 2 specific things I loved and appreciated about you this week.
2. **What Went Well (5 mins):** Moments of joy, teamwork, or peace in the home.
3. **What Can We Improve (10 mins):** One friction point and how we can support each other better.
4. **Financial Check-in (10 mins):** Review weekly spending against monthly budget.
5. **Spiritual Check-in (10 mins):** Qur'an progress, shared salah, upcoming charity.
6. **Closing Dua:** Make dua together for barakah and protection.
```
