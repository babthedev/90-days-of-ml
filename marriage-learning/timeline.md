## 📅 Phase 1 — Foundations: Marriage as Worship (Days 1–15)

**Goal:** Replace cultural fantasy with an Islamic vision of marriage; know rights and duties before knowing your spouse.

- [ ] **[[Day 02 - Why I want to marry|Day 02 (Fri Oct 2)]]**: **Why I want to marry** (W1 D3–4)

- [ ] **[[Day 03 - The Prophetic example- Khadijah|Day 03 (Sat Oct 3)]]**: **The Prophetic example: Khadijah** (W1 D5–6)
  - **Do (45 min):** The Prophet ﷺ and Khadijah: trust, respect, emotional safety. Talk: ask a married person, 'What did you believe about marriage that turned out false?'

- [ ] **[[Day 04 - The husband's duties & nafaqah|Day 04 (Sun Oct 4)]]**: **The husband's duties & nafaqah** (W1 D7 + W2 D1)

- [ ] **[[Day 05 - Wife's rights & prophetic qiwamah|Day 05 (Mon Oct 5)]]**: **Wife's rights & prophetic qiwamah** (W2 D2–3)
