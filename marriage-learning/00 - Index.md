## 🚩 Cultural Red Flags to Unlearn (Islamic vs Cultural)
- ❌ *"A man must never apologize"* → **The Sunnah teaches the opposite (humility, repair)**
- ❌ *The silent treatment / emotional withdrawal* → **Against the Sunnah (clear, gentle communication)**
- ❌ *Overspending on weddings to impress community* → **Destroys barakah; simplicity is prophetic**
- ❌ *Comparing your spouse to social media fantasies* → **Poisonous to contentment (qanaʿah)**

## 📅 The 6 Phases (15 Days Each)
1. **Phase 1 (Days 1–15):** Foundations: Marriage as Worship
2. **Phase 2 (Days 16–30):** Character & Self-Purification (Tazkiyah)
3. **Phase 3 (Days 31–45):** Fiqh of Marriage (Rulings, Rights & Boundaries)
4. **Phase 4 (Days 46–60):** Communication & Emotional Intelligence
5. **Phase 5 (Days 61–75):** Finance, Leadership & Life Planning
6. **Phase 6 (Days 76–90):** Spiritual Home & Marriage Covenant
