# 90-Day Islamic Marriage Preparation Curriculum 🕌

A structured 90-day journey (Oct 1 – Dec 29, 2026) to prepare spiritually, legally, emotionally, and financially for marriage based on the Qur'an, the Sunnah, and classical wisdom.

---

## 🎯 High-Level Vision
- **Daily Discipline:** 45 minutes (25m study, 15m write, 5m log).
- **Core Principle:** Replace cultural myths with Islamic clarity; know your obligations before demanding your rights.
- **Key Deliverables:** 
  - Intention Document ("Why I Want to Marry")
  - Personal Marriage Code
  - Conflict Resolution Code
  - Debt-Free Wedding & 12-Month Financial Plan
  - Weekly Marriage Meeting Template
  - Signed Marriage Covenant reviewed by an elder/imam.

---
