# The 10 Islamic Marriage Vocabulary Groups 📚

*Defined during the 6 phase review days. For each term: Arabic, English nuance, primary reference, and lived application.*

---

## Group 1: Spiritual Framework (Review Day 15)
- **ʿIbadah (عبادة):** Worship — viewing marital service, intimacy, and patience as acts of devotion.
- **Niyyah (نية):** Intention — purifying the goal of marriage for Allah's pleasure and chastity.
- **Taqwa (تقوى):** God-consciousness — the ultimate restraint when no one else is watching.
- **Sakinah (سكينة):** Tranquility — emotional peace and safety in the spouse's presence (30:21).
- **Mawaddah (مودة):** Active, expressed love shown through deeds and service (30:21).
- **Rahmah (رحمة):** Compassion and mercy — forgiving flaws and overlooking weaknesses (30:21).
- **Barakah (بركة):** Divine blessing — contentment and increase through adhering to the Sunnah.
- **Sabr (صبر):** Patience and self-restraint during friction or trials.
- **Shukr (شكر):** Gratitude — expressing thanks to Allah and openly appreciating the spouse.
- **Ihsan (إحسان):** Excellence — treating the spouse better than the bare legal minimum.
- **Husn al-Khuluq (حسن الخلق):** Beautiful character — the heaviest deed on the scales.
- **Amanah (أمانة):** Trust — recognizing the spouse and marriage as a sacred responsibility.
- **ʿAdl (عدل):** Justice and fairness — never taking advantage of one's legal authority.
- **Ghayrah (غيرة):** Protective jealousy — guarding the dignity, honor, and modesty of the family.
- **Haya' (حياء):** Modesty and shame before Allah and others.

---

## Group 4: Character & Tazkiyah (Review Day 30)
- **Tazkiyat an-Nafs (تزكية النفس):** Purification of the soul from pride, malice, and selfishness.
- **Mujahadah (مجاهدة):** Striving against personal desires and ego.
- **Riya' (رياء):** Showing off — seeking public praise rather than sincerity.
- **Hasad (حسد):** Destructive envy — resenting others' blessings.
- **Kibr (كبر):** Arrogance — rejecting the truth and looking down upon people.
- **Hilm (حلم):** Forbearance — remaining calm and composed during provocation.
- **ʿAfw (عفو):** Complete pardoning — wiping away the fault without grudge or retaliation.
- **Sadaqah (صدقة):** Charity — including every smile, chore, and provision for the family.
- **Tawbah (توبة):** Repentance and immediate turning back to Allah after a slip.

---

## Group 2: Fiqh of Nikah (Review Day 45)
- **Nikah (نكاح):** The marriage contract.
- **Wali (ولي):** The bride's guardian — protecting her interests, not coercing her.
- **Mahr (مهر):** The mandatory bridal gift given directly to the bride.
- **Ijab & Qabul (إيجاب وقبول):** The formal offer and acceptance.
- **Shuhud (شهود):** Two upright male witnesses.
- **Rukn & Shart (ركن وشرط):** Essential pillars and prerequisites of a valid contract.
- **Nafaqah (نفقة):** The husband's obligation to provide food, clothing, and housing.
- **Qiwamah (قوامة):** Prophetic guardianship, protection, and leadership with care.
- **Talaq, Khulʿ, ʿIddah (طلاق، خلع، عدة):** The legal mechanisms and waiting periods in divorce.
- **Sulh (صلح):** Amicable reconciliation and arbitration.

---

## Group 3: Intimacy & Modesty (Review Day 45)
- **Adab al-Jimaʿ (آداب الجماع):** Prophetic etiquette of intimacy — mutual rights, kindness, no harm.
- **Ghusl & Taharah (غسل وطهارة):** Ritual purification required after marital relations.
- **ʿAwrah & ʿIffah (عورة وعفة):** Boundaries of modesty and active pursuit of chastity.

---

## Group 6: Communication (Review Day 60)
- Active listening, emotional regulation, non-defensive communication, validation, empathy, repair attempts, I-statements, emotional safety.

---

## Group 8: Conflict & Stability (Review Day 60)
- Anger management, 24-hour time-out rule, reconciliation, arbitration (hakam), emotional flooding, ending resentment cycles.

---

## Group 7: Family Finance (Review Day 75)
- Halal income, rizq, tawakkul, avoiding israf (wastefulness), qanaʿah (contentment), emergency funds, avoiding riba and debt.

---

## Group 9: Parenting & Vision (Review Day 75)
- Tarbiyah (nurturing faith), children as an amanah, passing on aqeedah, discipline vs abuse, breaking intergenerational trauma.

---

## Group 5: The Prophetic Model (Review Day 90)
- Prophetic emotional intelligence, playfulness with dignity (racing Aisha), listening, patience in hardship, handling jealousy.

---

## Group 10: Marriage Architecture (Review Day 90)
- Covenant vs contract, rights vs responsibilities, authority vs tyranny, love vs mercy, complementarity, culture vs Shariʿah, "from me to us".
